# Dental Care Clinic System — User Manual

This guide explains how patients and clinic team members use the Dental Care system. You do not need technical knowledge to follow it. The exact options you see depend on your account and the clinic's current schedule.

## The visit process at a glance

**Patient:** Create account → verify email → sign in → request an appointment → wait for clinic confirmation → visit the clinic and check in → attend the consultation → review your records and bill.

**Clinic team:** Review appointment requests → confirm or decline → check in the patient → manage the waiting queue → record the visit → prepare the bill → record payment and provide the receipt.

## 1. Patients

### Create your patient account

1. Open the clinic website address provided by the clinic.
2. Select **Sign in**, then **Create an account**.
3. Enter your name, date of birth, gender, contact number, email address, and home address. An emergency contact is optional.
4. Create a password of at least 12 characters and enter it again to confirm.
5. Select **Create patient account**.
6. Check your email for a six-digit verification code. Enter the code on the verification page and select **Verify Email**. The code expires after 15 minutes.
7. If the code does not arrive, check your junk or spam folder. You can select **Resend Code** when that option becomes available.

Use an email address you can access. If verification still does not work, contact the clinic.

### Sign in

1. Open the clinic website and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter the email address and password you used when registering.
3. Select **Sign in**. Your **Overview** page shows your next appointment, outstanding bills, and recent dental records.

Patient accounts are for patients. Clinic staff and doctor accounts are provided by the clinic administrator.

### Request an appointment

1. From **Overview**, select **Book or manage appointments**, or open **Appointments**.
2. In **Request an appointment**, choose the clinic branch.
3. Choose the dental treatment or treatments you want to request. Review any displayed fee information; the clinic confirms the final service details.
4. Choose a date with available times, then select an available appointment time. The calendar and time list show the options currently open for that branch.
5. Submit the request.
6. Return to **Appointments** to check its status. A request marked **Pending** has not yet been confirmed. Wait until it is **Approved** before treating the appointment as confirmed.

The schedule uses 30-minute appointment slots, Monday through Saturday, from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM, with a lunch break from 12:00 PM to 1:00 PM. The clinic is closed on Sundays. Available dates and times can change as appointments are booked or the clinic updates its schedule.

If the request is **Rejected**, contact the clinic to discuss another time. If **Cancel** is available beside an appointment, you can use it to cancel a pending or approved appointment. Contact the clinic if you need help changing an appointment.

### Visit the clinic

1. Arrive at the branch and appointment time confirmed by the clinic.
2. Tell the clinic team your name so they can check you in.
3. If you have registered an RFID card and the clinic has activated it, the team may use it to identify you at check-in. An RFID card is optional and is not used to sign in to your account.
4. Wait for the clinic team to call you. The clinic team manages the waiting queue and will let you know when your visit begins.

### Review dental records and bills

- Select **Dental records** to view treatment history, diagnoses, prescriptions, notes, and any recorded follow-up date.
- Select **My billing** to review bills, services, totals, payments, and remaining balances.
- For a payment with a receipt, select **Preview Payment Receipt**.
- The receipt generated by this system is an **Acknowledgement Receipt only**. It is not an Official Receipt or a BIR-compliant Sales Invoice. Ask the clinic if you need clarification about your payment document.
- The patient portal lets you review billing information and receipts. The clinic team records payments in the system; contact the clinic to arrange or make a payment.
- Select **Notifications** to review clinic messages.

### Register an RFID card (optional)

1. Open **My RFID card**.
2. Scan or enter the UID printed on the card, then select **Register RFID card**.
3. The clinic manages card activation and validity. Registering a card does not replace your email and password when signing in.

If you replace your card, update the registered UID. The replacement UID becomes the one linked to your account.

### Sign out

Use **Sign out** when you have finished, especially on a shared device.

## 2. Clinic team

Clinic team members sign in with an account created by the clinic administrator. The pages and actions available depend on whether the account is for staff, a doctor, or an administrator. Staff and doctor accounts may be limited to their assigned branch.

### Review appointment requests

1. Sign in and open **Appointments**.
2. Find pending requests. Use the branch, date, status, or search filters if needed.
3. Review the patient's requested branch, treatment, date, and time.
4. Select **Approve** to confirm the appointment or **Reject** if the clinic cannot accept it.
5. To change a future appointment time, use the appointment's rescheduling option and select an available time.

Patients can see the appointment status in their portal. Approval and rejection may also trigger a clinic notification.

### Register a walk-in

1. Open **Appointments** and find **Register a walk-in**.
2. Select the branch, patient, treatment, date, and available time.
3. Submit the form. Walk-ins are added to the waiting queue immediately.
4. If the patient does not have an account in the system, add the patient from **Patients** first.

### Check in a patient

Use either of these options:

- **Patient queue:** Find the approved appointment in **Patient queue** and select **Check in**.
- **RFID check-in:** Open **RFID check-in**, then scan the patient's activated card or enter its UID and follow the on-screen result.

Check-ins appear in that day's queue. An approved appointment can be checked in from the queue even if it was scheduled for another day; the check-in joins today's queue.

### Manage the waiting queue

1. Open **Patient queue**.
2. When the patient is ready, select **Call patient**.
3. When the consultation begins, select **Start visit**.
4. If a checked-in patient does not attend, eligible staff can mark the appointment **No show**.

The queue page refreshes periodically. Confirm the patient before advancing their visit.

### Record the consultation

1. During a visit, open the appointment's **Dental record** action.
2. Enter the diagnosis and treatment. Add a prescription, notes, or follow-up date when appropriate.
3. Select **Save dental record**.

A dental record must be saved before a bill can be created for the appointment.

### Create a bill and record payment

1. After the dental record is saved, open **Create bill** for the appointment.
2. Review the service items and amounts carefully. Apply any appropriate discount and confirm the final bill amount.
3. Select **Create bill**.
4. In **Billing & payments**, find the bill and check its total and remaining balance.
5. When payment is received, choose the payment method and enter the amount received and the amount applied to the bill. For cash, the amount received may be greater than the amount applied; the difference is recorded as change. For other methods, enter the exact amount being applied.
6. Select **Record payment**. The system updates the bill and generates an Acknowledgement Receipt.
7. Open or print the receipt for the patient. Remind the patient that it is not an Official Receipt or a BIR-compliant Sales Invoice.

Partial payments are allowed. The appointment is marked completed after treatment is recorded and the bill is fully paid. If a later add-on service creates a balance, the appointment may return to billed status until the balance is paid.

## 3. Clinic administrators

Administrators have additional clinic-management pages:

- **Staff & doctor accounts:** Create staff or doctor accounts, assign their branch, enable or disable access, and reset passwords.
- **Services:** Maintain the clinic's service list and pricing.
- **Appointment availability:** Review or update schedule availability.
- **Reports:** Review clinic activity and revenue information.
- **Audit logs and SMS logs:** Review recorded system actions and message attempts.

Only administrators should create or manage clinic staff accounts. Give each team member their own account and do not share passwords.

## 4. Common questions

**My appointment says Pending. Is it confirmed?**  
No. Pending means the clinic still needs to review the request. Wait for the status to change to Approved or contact the clinic.

**I cannot find a suitable appointment time.**  
Try another date or branch. Only times the clinic currently makes available are selectable. Contact the clinic if you need help.

**I cannot sign in or I forgot my password.**  
Check that you entered the same email address used to register. Patient password recovery is handled by the clinic; contact the clinic for help. Clinic team members should contact their administrator.

**I do not see a dental record or bill.**  
The clinic team must enter the visit record or bill before it appears in your portal. Contact the clinic if you think something is missing.

**My payment receipt says Acknowledgement Receipt.**  
That is the type of receipt this system creates. It is not an Official Receipt or a BIR-compliant Sales Invoice. Ask the clinic about other documentation.

**I have a problem with my appointment or account.**  
Contact the clinic directly. Do not send your password to anyone.
